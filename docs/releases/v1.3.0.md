# AI PM Skills v1.3.0

**Evals as evidence.** The same 29 Agent Skills for AI product managers who build, in regulated European companies. In this release every skill, legal ones included, is graded by blind judges from three vendors, and the evals chart shows that judge's gain next to the regex gain. Every skill has a recorded eval run on exactly the text you download, and passes it.

## New in v1.3.0

### Legal skills judged too

The six legal skills were left out of the judges so far. They are now graded on request (`judge_answers.py --include-legal`) by GPT-6.1 Sol, Jev and Haiku 5.5, on the same rubric as the other skills. This is information only: the rubric does not check legal correctness, a legal skill's pass rule never includes a judge, and counsel's review stays the evidence for these skills.

### `ai-act-classification` 0.4.0

Revised from the judges' critiques and an independent legal review checked against published sources:

- Emotion recognition, prohibited at work and in education, means inferring emotions from biometric data (art. 3(39), art. 5(1)(f)): face, voice, posture or keystrokes count; sentiment read from written text alone does not.
- The use sets the Annex III category at every autonomy level: credit scoring is Annex III point 5(b) with or without human review. A step that relied on the art. 6(3) exception can lose it when it moves up a level. The autonomy levels are the team's own scale, not a legal category.
- Art. 26(5): on a risk, inform the provider or distributor and the market surveillance authority and suspend use; a serious incident goes to the provider first, then to the importer or distributor and the authority. Art. 26(9): the provider's information feeds the deployer's data protection impact assessment.
- The memo states the dates as last checked, never an invented "today", and names documents and evidence, not this repo's skills or files.

Rerun on Claude, 3 runs per case: 100% on every case, red lines held.

GPT-6.1 Sol still scores the answers with the skill lower than those without. Most of its critiques mark down the 2026 amendments it does not know: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the Digital Omnibus on AI), Annex III high-risk from 2 December 2027, the art. 111(4) transition. The legal review confirmed them, so the skill keeps them. A general-purpose judge does not check the law; it grades what it believes.
