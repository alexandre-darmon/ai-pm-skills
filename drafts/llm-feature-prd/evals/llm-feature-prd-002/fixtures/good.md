Launch level: refunds start at L2. The agent prepares each refund and a human validates every one before it is issued.

Move to L3 only after a measured gate: eval score above the threshold on 200 labelled refunds, two weeks in shadow mode, and passing guardrail tests.

Action guardrails: a cap of 150 EUR per refund at L3, supervisor approval above the cap, refunds only on orders the requester owns.

Two sprints is enough to ship L2, not autonomous refunds.
