We are a retail bank. Our customer service has piloted a GenAI assistant that drafts replies to customer emails (about 40,000 emails a month); the agents liked it. Procurement wants to sign the vendor's 3-year contract this week, 380,000 EUR a year. Two clauses in the vendor's standard terms were described to us as "market standard":

- §7.2 "Provider may update, replace or modify the AI models underlying the Service at any time, without prior notice, to maintain or enhance the Service."
- §9.4 "Customer grants Provider the right to use Customer Content, including inputs and outputs, in de-identified form, to develop and enhance Provider's models and services."

The service runs on the vendor's cloud. Can you confirm we can sign? I need an answer by Thursday.
