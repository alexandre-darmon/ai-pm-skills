Pair every usage panel with a quality line next to it: sessions alone say nothing about whether the answers were right.

- Quality by domain (claims, contracts): share of answers judged correct in a weekly sampled review of 60 conversations per domain, graded by claims handlers. The thumbs-up rate is not quality.
- Cost per correct outcome = 4,160 EUR ÷ (volume × quality). At 70% correct, that is 4,160 ÷ (52,000 × 0.70) ≈ 0.11 EUR, not 0.08 EUR.
- Volume by use case (claims, contracts), from traces and classification.
- Adoption: active users ÷ eligible policyholders by population (individual, business), weekly curve.
- Stale-answer rate by KB: share of sampled answers relying on a superseded document.
