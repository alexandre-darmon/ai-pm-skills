Twelve real questions cannot fill 150 cases, but they can ground the synthetic ones.

1. Dimensions derived from the 12 questions: intent (early repayment, rate change, payment holiday, loan insurance, documents, sale or full repayment, death of a co-borrower), persona (borrower, heir, notary), language and style (French without accents, terse English), edge condition (life event, missing loan details). Each value points to one of the 12; anything else is marked assumed.
2. List tuples first, one value per dimension, weighting the risky ones (death, job loss) above their traffic share. Advisers review the tuple table.
3. Then, in a separate step, write one message per tuple with the real questions as style anchors.
4. Advisers review every input and reject what a customer would not write; record the rejection rate per value and regenerate above 20%.
5. Expected answers are written by advisers from the product terms, never by the assistant.
6. Flag each case as synthetic (origin, generator, date) and state the synthetic share per class: about 138 of 150 today.
7. Replacement plan: after launch, replace synthetic cases with real masked conversations, aiming for under 50% synthetic per class by three months after launch.
