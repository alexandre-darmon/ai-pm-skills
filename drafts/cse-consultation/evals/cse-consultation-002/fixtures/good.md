With 9 employees you have no CSE: it is mandatory only from 11 employees for 12 consecutive months (L2311-2). There is no consultation to run.

What applies anyway:
- The AI note-taker records employees: tell them before any data about them is collected (L1222-4), and keep the recording justified and proportionate (L1121-1).
- Good practice: inform the team in writing before you start, explain what is recorded and who can see it.
- The recordings are personal data: check the GDPR basis and whether an impact assessment is needed, and follow the CNIL guidance.
- When you reach 11 employees for 12 consecutive months, a CSE becomes mandatory; consultation on new technologies and monitoring tools starts only from 50 employees (L2312-8, L2312-38).

HR and legal decide; this memo prepares their decision.
