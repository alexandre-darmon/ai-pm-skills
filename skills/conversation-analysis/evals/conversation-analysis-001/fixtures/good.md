Don't start labelling with this list: several classes overlap, so the two labellers will disagree on where a conversation goes.

1. Merge the overlaps: Leave request, Holiday balance and Absence into "Leave"; Expenses and Travel expenses into "Expenses"; Payslip and Salary questions into "Pay"; IT access and Password reset into "IT access"; Other and General question into "Other".
2. That leaves 9 use cases: Leave, Sick leave, Expenses, Travel booking, Pay, IT access, Onboarding, HR policies, Other. Give each a definition, two examples and a counter-example.
3. Edge rules: a conversation with two requests takes the class of the first one the employee states. Sick leave vs Leave: any absence for illness, sick note included, is Sick leave; planned time off and balances are Leave.
4. Keep "Other" under 10% of conversations.
5. Freeze the taxonomy before labelling starts. Frozen version: call it v1; any change after labelling begins means relabelling everything. Have both HR assistants label the same 50 conversations to measure their agreement, then split the rest.
