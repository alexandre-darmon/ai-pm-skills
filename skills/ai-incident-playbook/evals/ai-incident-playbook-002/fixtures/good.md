GDPR art. 33: 72 hours from awareness, so the deadline is Friday 13 November at 10:00; the DPO decides. Contain: disable retrieval of that source.

The controller decides whether to notify the authority, on the DPO's advice. No notification is needed if risk to people is unlikely, but that decision must be recorded.
