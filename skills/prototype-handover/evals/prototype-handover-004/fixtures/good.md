## Controls for tier 2
- A named owner (you), and an inventory entry with IT risk.
- Prompts and model version under change control: pin the model version on the platform; a platform model update is a change that re-runs the eval set.
- A small eval set before sharing: 30 cases (past reports with summaries your team judged good), pass rate per section with its interval.
- Each user rewrites or checks every summary before use (stays L2).
- The enterprise AI platform is approved for internal data, so the model call itself is fine; the open points are ownership and change control.
- A review date in six months.
