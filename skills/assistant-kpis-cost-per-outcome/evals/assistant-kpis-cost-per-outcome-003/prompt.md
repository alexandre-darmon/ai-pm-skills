- HR policies: 240 documents, all reviewed in the last 3 months.
- IT procedures: 180 documents, last full review 14 months ago; the ticketing tool changed in the spring.

Last quarter, a review of 200 answers found 84% correct overall. About 9,000 conversations a month, 3,500 eligible employees in 3 countries, 1,900 EUR a month in run costs.
