12 correct answers out of 12 reads as 100%, but 12 cases cannot demonstrate a 95% bar. The 95% Wilson interval for 12 out of 12 runs from about 76% to 100%: the true accuracy could be well under 95% and still produce this run. Put differently, zero failures in 12 cases still allows a failure rate up to about 25% (rule of three, 3/n).

For a class where a false pass costs a labour-court case, the gate passes when the lower bound clears the threshold. To show 95% with no failure you need at least 60 clean cases (3/60 = 5%); if a few failures are expected, plan for about 75 to 140 cases.
