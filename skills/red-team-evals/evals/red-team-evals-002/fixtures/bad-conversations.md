I would not write "robust": 50 prompts cannot support it.

- Zero successes in 50 still allows a true rate up to about 6% (rule of three, 3/50; Wilson about 7%). At 25,000 conversations a month, that could still mean around 1,500 successful attacks a month.
- The prompts came from a well-known public list, likely already patched, and were chosen and run by the build team, not independent testers.
- Only direct jailbreaks were tried: indirect prompt injection and another customer's data were not.

Proposed wording: "Direct jailbreaks: 0 of 50 public prompts succeeded (95% upper bound about 7%). Not tested: indirect injection, cross-customer data access."
