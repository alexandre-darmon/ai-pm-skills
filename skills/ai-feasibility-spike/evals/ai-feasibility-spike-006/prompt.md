Our head of treasury wants "an AI" to decide each week which supplier invoices we pay first, to protect our cash position and avoid late-payment penalties. Today two people do it in a spreadsheet with a few rules (due date, early-payment discount, strategic suppliers), about 6 hours a week. We have three years of payment history in the ERP but nobody has looked at its quality.

Nothing has been built and I don't want anyone building yet. Before we spend anything, is this even a problem for AI, or would better rules do? I need a one-page view for the executive committee next month.
