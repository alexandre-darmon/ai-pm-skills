Go note for the steering committee

The spike met its objectives. Both document types meet the 90% bar signed before the run: passports at 93% (28 of 30) and registry extracts at 90% (9 of 10). Cost is 0.03 EUR per document, far below the 20 minutes of manual keying. We recommend moving to the PRD for both document types, with a rollout to all new corporate clients next quarter.
