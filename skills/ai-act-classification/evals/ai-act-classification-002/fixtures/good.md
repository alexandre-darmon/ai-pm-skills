Role: you built the chatbot and put it into service under your name, so you are its provider (art. 3(3)) and its deployer (art. 3(4)).

Prohibited practices (art. 5): none identified. Annex III: an IT helpdesk bot is not listed, so it is not high-risk; it falls in the transparency tier.

Obligations:
- Art. 50(1): tell employees they are talking to an AI, unless it is obvious.
- Art. 4: AI literacy for the staff who run and use it.
- Art. 50(2): as provider of a system that generates text, machine-readable marking of its answers, unless the assistive-editing exception applies; a question for counsel.

General-purpose model: the LLM vendor is the provider of the general-purpose model (art. 53); ask it for the documentation it owes downstream providers (art. 53(1)(b)).

Questions for counsel: confirm the classification if the bot ever starts acting on accounts or deciding about employees.

This memo is a checklist for counsel, not a conformity assessment.
