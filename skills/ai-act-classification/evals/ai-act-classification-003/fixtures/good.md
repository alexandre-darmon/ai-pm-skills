| Step | Today → target | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address change, card block, card limit | L2 → L4 | Still outside Annex III; art. 50(1) applies (customers told they deal with an AI). |
| Overdraft up to 500 EUR | L2 → L4 | If the chatbot assesses the customer's creditworthiness, this step is Annex III point 5(b), high-risk, at any level; the art. 6(3) exception cannot apply because it profiles. The move to L4 removes the adviser check, so the oversight duties tighten. |

Obligations that appear for the overdraft step (you as deployer, art. 26): human oversight by competent people with authority (art. 26(2), designed in by the provider under art. 14); logs kept at least six months (art. 26(6)); customers informed that the system decides (art. 26(11)) and entitled to an explanation (art. 86); a fundamental rights impact assessment before first use (art. 27).

Questions for counsel: does the chatbot assess creditworthiness, or apply a limit set by the bank's credit engine? Did the bank build the chatbot (then it is also provider: arts. 9-17, 43, 49) or buy it? When do arts. 72-73 and 86 bite?

Dates: art. 50 applies since 2 August 2026; the Annex III duties for the overdraft step (arts. 6-27) apply from 2 December 2027 (art. 113 as amended in 2026).
