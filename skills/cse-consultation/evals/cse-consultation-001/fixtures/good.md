Regime: about 300 employees, so the CSE has full information and consultation duties (L2312-8) and a CSSCT (L2315-36).

Obligation: information and consultation on the introduction of a new technology that changes the advisers' work (L2312-8, II, 4°). Monitoring screen: if the assistant logs prompts or acceptance rates per adviser, add the consultation on means of monitoring activity (L2312-38) and inform advisers beforehand (L1222-4).

The pilot with 40 advisers counts as deployment (TJ Nanterre, 14 Feb. 2025): it cannot start before the CSE has given its opinion. Deploying without consultation is a délit d'entrave (L2317-1, fine up to EUR 7,500, EUR 37,500 for the company).

File (L2312-15), put in the BDESE (L2312-18): purpose, functioning, data, impact on tasks and workload, training plan.

Timeline (R2312-6, absent an agreement): one month for the opinion, two months if the CSE appoints an expert (L2315-94); the CSSCT prepares the work but does not extend the delay. Plan the pilot after the opinion, so March is tight.

Question for legal on anything planned before the opinion, with the facts: who uses it and how many of the 140 advisers; real work or test data; whether drafts reach real clients; whether a consultation is already open. These facts do not make a pilot safe.

HR and legal decide; this memo prepares their decision.
