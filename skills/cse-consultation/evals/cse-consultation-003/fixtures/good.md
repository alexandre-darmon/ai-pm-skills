The dashboard can monitor individual handlers, and the capability is what matters, even if management does not intend to use it to evaluate anyone (Cass. soc., 11 Dec. 2019, n° 18-11.792). Generative AI is also a new technology under L2312-8 (TJ Créteil 2025, upheld by CA Paris, 21 May 2026).

- Consultation on a means of monitoring employees' activity, before the decision (L2312-38), on top of the consultation on a new technology (L2312-8, II, 4°).
- Handlers informed before any data about them is collected (L1222-4); the per-handler view must be justified and proportionate (L1121-1).
- Safer design: switch off the per-handler view or aggregate it at team level before the pilot.
- File: say what happens when a handler types sensitive content into the tool (their health, harassment, a dispute), who can read it and where it is routed.
- Personal data: check the GDPR impact assessment and the CNIL guidance on employee monitoring.
