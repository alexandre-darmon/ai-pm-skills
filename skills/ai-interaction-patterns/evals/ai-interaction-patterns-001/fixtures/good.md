Edit-first: the draft opens editable with its sources, each statement linked to its span in the transcript. Statements with no source are labelled "not found in the transcript" and each one needs an action (keep, edit or delete) before the save is enabled. The Save to CRM control sits in the editor and writes the adviser's version, never the raw draft in one click. No "I have reviewed this" checkbox: it gets ticked unread.

Correction in one action: edit inline or mark a line wrong; feedback is logged with the original and corrected text and goes into the eval set. A high no-edit rate with very short review time would suggest over-trust: check it with seeded errors and a sampled review.

An "I have reviewed this" checkbox, a confirm dialog or a sign-off button gets clicked unread and proves nothing. We considered a confirmation checkbox and rejected it.
